## 🦷 Overview

**Ordent Studio** is a comprehensive, multi-page web platform designed for a premier modern dental practice and educational institute. Combining patient care, academic excellence, clinical research, and dental supplies, Ordent Studio offers an end-to-end digital experience for patients, dental practitioners, and dental students.

The website delivers a high-performance, mobile-responsive user interface with interactive animations (AOS), clear navigation, and dedicated inquiry & appointment booking channels.

## ✨ Key Features & Page Directory

### 1. 🏠 Homepage (`index.html`)
- **Brand Top Bar**: Quick social links, clinic branding, and direct contact numbers.
- **Hero Section**: Engaging imagery, brand slogan (*"Bringing Perfect Smiles to Life"*), and call-to-action buttons.
- **Key Information Strip**: Hours, phone, emergency line, location, and primary/alternative emails.
- **Services Showcase**: Detailed overview of dental offerings (Orthodontics, Whitening, Implants, Restorative care).
- **Interactive Reviews Carousel**: Patient testimonials and success stories.
- **Footer**: Integrated links, about summary, and unified contact details.

### 2. 🎓 Professional Education
A dedicated academic hub for continuing dental education:
- **Courses (`courses.html`)**: Dynamic filterable catalog of clinical workshops, implantology certifications, and pediatric care programs.
- **Faculty Directory (`faculty.html`)**: Profiles of specialized surgeons, orthodontists, prosthodontists, and clinical instructors.
- **Case Studies (`casestudy.html`)**: Real clinical case breakdowns with diagnosis, treatment protocols, and post-op outcomes.
- **Help Documents (`helpdoc.html`)**: Interactive accordion FAQs covering licensing, enrollment, course schedules, and clinical requirements.
- **Student Resources (`student.html`)**: Curated toolkits, clinical manuals, downloadable PDFs, and student support.

### 3. 🛡️ Patient Education (`patient.html`)
- Comprehensive guides on oral hygiene, cavities prevention, gum care, braces maintenance, and emergency dental steps.
- Interactive FAQ system addressing common patient questions and post-treatment guidance.

### 4. 🔬 Research Hub (`research.html`)
- Overview of foundational and clinical dental research initiatives.
- **Research Day**: Information, keynote highlights, and abstract submission portal (`research@ordentstudio.com`).
- Downloadable clinical toolkits and procedure video libraries.

### 5. 🛍️ Dental Products (`product.html`)
- Catalog of recommended oral health care items, precision dental equipment, and hygiene accessories.

### 6. 📞 Contact & Appointments (`ContactUs.html`)
- **Primary & Alternative Contact Cards**: Direct phone, alternative WhatsApp number, primary email, and booking desk.
- **Directions & Transit Guide**: Bus routes and landmark navigation tips.
- **Google Maps Integration**: Accurate map embed centered on Karachi location.
- **Interactive Booking Form**: Name, email, phone, custom message input, and newsletter preferences.

### 7. 🗺️ Site Map (`Site.html`)
- Organized layout linking every page and educational category for easy indexing and accessible navigation.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Markup:** HTML5 (Semantic structure, modern metadata, SEO title hierarchy)
- **Styling:** CSS3 (Custom styles `home.css`, `site.css`, `faculty.css`)
- **Framework:** Bootstrap 5.3.3 (Responsive grid, modals, carousels, navbars)
- **Icons:** Font Awesome 6.7.2
- **Animations:** AOS (Animate On Scroll) Library
- **Scripting:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) for interactive UI, accordions, and course filters
